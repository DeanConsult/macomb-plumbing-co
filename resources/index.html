6 Plumbing Tips Every Homeowner Should Know

Know Where Your Shutoff Valve Is

Locate your main water shutoff valve before a leak happens. It’s usually in the basement or utility room near where the water line enters your home. Turning it off quickly can prevent thousands in water damage.

Never Pour Grease Down the Drain

Grease and cooking oil solidify in pipes over time and cause serious blockages. Dispose of grease in a container and throw it in the trash. Use drain strainers to catch food particles before they reach your pipes.

Don’t Ignore a Dripping Faucet

A dripping faucet wastes over 3,000 gallons of water per year and is often a sign of a worn seal or valve that will eventually fail completely. Fix it early before it becomes a bigger and more expensive problem.

Protect Pipes from Freezing

Michigan winters can freeze exposed pipes in garages, crawl spaces, and exterior walls. Insulate them before cold weather hits. During extreme cold, let faucets drip slightly to keep water moving and prevent bursting.

Flush Your Water Heater Annually

Sediment builds up in water heater tanks over time, reducing efficiency and shortening the unit’s lifespan. An annual flush keeps your water heater running efficiently and can add years to its life.

Watch What You Flush

Only toilet paper belongs in the toilet. “Flushable” wipes, paper towels, cotton products, and hygiene items do not break down and are a leading cause of sewer line blockages. When in doubt, throw it in the trash.
