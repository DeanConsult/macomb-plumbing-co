Our Plumbing Services

Macomb Plumbing is excellent at any type of plumbing repair or installation — residential and commercial. Call if you don’t see what you’re looking for.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged drain repair, clogged toilet repair, floor drain back-up, and grease trap repair. We use advanced equipment — including camera scopes — to find and fix your drain problem safely and economically without damaging your pipes.

Pipe Leak Repair & Replacement

Pipe leaks and cracked pipes can cause serious water damage fast. Our plumbers repair or replace any pipe — water lines, sewer lines, water mains, and frozen or burst pipes — and get your water service operational quickly.

Water Heater Repair & Replacement

We service all makes and models of water heaters. Most repairs and replacements are completed in a single visit. We carry parts on our trucks to get your hot water back on as fast as possible.

Toilet Repair & Replacement

Broken toilet repair and replacement, clogged toilet repair, running toilet repair. We install new toilets from American Standard, Kohler, Moen, and other major brands with a written estimate provided upfront.

Faucet & Fixture Repair

Leaky faucet repair and replacement, shower and tub faucet repair, garbage disposal repair and installation, insta-hot units, refrigerator water lines, and new appliance installation. We work with Delta, Kohler, Moen, and Grohe.

Emergency Plumbing

We offer 24-hour emergency plumbing service every day of the year. If you have a plumbing emergency after hours, call us. We have plumbers standing by to be dispatched to your home or business quickly.

Sewer Line & Water Main

Sewer line installation, water main repairs, leaking valve repair and replacement. We use camera inspection technology to quickly diagnose the scope and location of sewer and drain problems — both residential and commercial.

Plumbing Installations

New sink, toilet, shower/tub, hot water heater, garbage disposal, insta-hot unit, and refrigerator water line installations. We work with most major brand name manufacturers and provide a full estimate before work begins.

Commercial Plumbing

We handle commercial plumbing repair and installations as comfortably as residential. We offer evening and weekend appointments to minimize disruption to your business. Offices, restaurants, retail stores, warehouses, and more.
