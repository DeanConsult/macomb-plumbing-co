Our Story

Macomb’s Most Trusted Plumbing Company

Macomb Plumbing has over 20 years of experience in all areas of plumbing. We have an excellent reputation in Macomb Michigan as well as throughout the entire Metro Detroit area. We receive calls daily from previous customers and referrals which tells us we must be doing something right.

Our entire staff is friendly and very knowledgeable in most aspects of the plumbing industry. We handle both residential and commercial plumbing repair and installations and offer 24-hour emergency service for those who have plumbing problems after hours.

In December 2020, we were honored by Metro Detroit Review as one of the top plumbing companies in the Detroit area. In June 2021, we were recognized by The Michigan Review Board and placed in the top 100 service companies in the Metro Detroit area. We are proud of these awards and work every day to live up to them.
