What We Do

Plumbing Services in Macomb MI

Macomb Plumbing is excellent at any type of plumbing repair or installation. We service both residential and commercial customers. If you don’t see what you need, just call us.

Drain Cleaning

Clogged drains, sinks, floor drains, and basement backups cleared fast. We use advanced equipment to unclog your drain safely without damaging your pipes. Camera inspection technology locates the problem quickly and accurately.

Pipe Leak Repair

Cracked or leaking pipes can cause serious damage fast. Our experienced plumbers repair or replace any pipe — including frozen and burst pipes from Michigan winters — and get your water service back on quickly.

Water Heater Repair & Replacement

We service all makes and models of water heaters. Minor repairs and full replacements completed in a single visit. We carry parts on our trucks so most jobs are done the same day you call.

Toilet Repair & Replacement

Broken, clogged, or running toilet — we handle it all. We also install new toilets from Kohler, American Standard, Moen, and more. Fast, clean work with a written estimate before we begin.

Faucet & Fixture Repair

Leaky faucets, shower and tub faucet repair, garbage disposal repair and installation, instant hot installation, new appliance installation. We work with all major brands including Delta, Kohler, Moen, and Grohe.

Emergency Plumbing

Plumbing problems don’t wait for business hours. Macomb Plumbing offers 24-hour emergency service throughout Macomb and Metro Detroit. Call us any time — day, night, weekends, and holidays.
